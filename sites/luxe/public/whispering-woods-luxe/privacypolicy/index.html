Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026 · Harvard, Illinois
Terms of Service · Heirloom collection

1. Who we are

Whispering Woods Luxe ("we," "us") operates the estate senior portrait experience and keepsake ordering at whisperingwoodsluxe.com and related pages including /heirloom, /terms, and this policy.

2. Information we collect

You provide when booking the estate experience:

  • Senior name and email
  • Parent or guardian name, email, and phone
  • Preferred experience week and session preferences
  • Terms acceptance and optional model-release choices

You provide when ordering heirlooms (/heirloom, Chalet kiosk, QR):

  • Contact name and email (matched to booking when possible)
  • Senior first name and booking reference
  • Product selections, cover preference, and design notes
  • Terms acceptance at checkout

Waitlist: email address only.

Automatic collection: IP address, browser or device type, pages viewed, timestamps, and basic error logs for security and operations. Payment card data is collected and processed by Stripe; we receive tokens and transaction status, not full card numbers.

Session images: photographs and videos created during your estate session, stored for delivery, retouching, and album production.

3. How we use information

  • Book, confirm, and coordinate your estate session
  • Process deposits, balances, and keepsake payments
  • Produce and fulfill albums, prints, and digital galleries
  • Send confirmations, reminders, balance-due notices, and delivery updates
  • Operate the Chalet order tablet and QR order flow
  • Prevent fraud, enforce our Terms, and defend payment disputes
  • Marketing and portfolio use only as described in our Terms and your opt-out choices
We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for unrelated advertising profiles.

4. How we share information

  • Stripe for payment processing
  • Booking and order backend (e.g., Xano or equivalent) for roster and order records
  • Production partners (album labs, framers) with the minimum data needed to fulfill your order (names, images, product specs). Partners are not authorized to use your data for their own marketing.
  • Service providers that host, email, or support our systems under confidentiality obligations
  • Authorities when required by law
  • Successors in a merger or sale of the business

5. Payment and consent records

When you pay or accept terms, we may store an audit record including email, order SKUs, amount, terms version, timestamp, IP address, user agent, and Stripe session or payment intent identifiers. These records support receipts, customer service, and chargeback defense, similar to industry practice for e-commerce and custom goods.

6. Retention

We retain booking and order data as long as needed to fulfill services, meet legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes. Session images are retained for delivery and reprint windows described at booking, then archived or deleted per our studio policy.

7. Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Report concerns to bookings@whisperingwoodsluxe.com.

8. Your choices

  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing bookings@whisperingwoodsluxe.com
  • Opt out of marketing image use in writing before your session (see Terms)
  • Note: Deleting booking data may cancel your session and forfeits a non-refundable deposit. Deleting data after keepsake payment may delay fulfillment.

9. Children and seniors

Our service is directed to families booking senior portrait sessions. We collect senior information only with parent or guardian involvement. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

10. Cookies and analytics

The estate marketing site (WeWeb) may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. The git-hosted /heirloom page uses minimal client-side storage for the current session only unless you complete checkout. See your browser settings to limit cookies.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above changes when revisions are posted. Continued use after an update constitutes acknowledgment.
