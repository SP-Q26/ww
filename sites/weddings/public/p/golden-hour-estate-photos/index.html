Golden hour on forty acres: ponds, pines, and meadow light

How to place portraits on a private Harvard estate without losing the whole party.

Save golden hour for two small groups, not the entire guest list. The estate has depth · use it in layers.

Spring: soft green and long shadows near the tree line. Summer: hazy backlight at the meadow. Fall: warm barn wood and amber leaves.

Give your photographer five minutes alone on tour to scout · they will thank you on the wedding day.

Pin this for your shot list; tour when you are ready to see real angles on three ponds and open meadow.
