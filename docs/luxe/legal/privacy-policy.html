Privacy Policy

Whispering Woods Luxe · Harvard, Illinois · Effective September 5, 2026 · Terms of Service

1. Data We Collect

When you book The Estate Senior Experience, we collect:

  • Senior Name
  • Senior Email
  • Mom Email
  • Phone Number
  • Preferred Week

Waitlist signups collect email address only.

2. How We Use Your Data

This information is used solely for booking, communication, and event coordination, including confirmations, reminders, balance due notices, and session logistics.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

  • Your information is stored securely in our booking system (Xano).
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties except as required for payment processing (Stripe).
  • Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.

4. Model Release & Marketing Images

If you grant model release consent at booking, selected session images may be used for portfolio, website, social media, and marketing as described in our Terms of Service. You may opt out in writing before your session date.

5. Your Choices

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing bookings@whisperingwoodsluxe.com. Note: Deleting your data will cancel your booking and forfeit your non-refundable deposit.

6. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after an update constitutes acknowledgment. The effective date above will change when revisions are posted.

Questions? Contact bookings@whisperingwoodsluxe.com
