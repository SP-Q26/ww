## The play (strategy)

1. **Base $1,420** · the estate day. Full price. No haggling.
2. **Keepsakes at market retail** · what they would pay a top North Shore studio for album, digital, and frame **separately**, without the estate experience.
3. **Chalet Collection pre-order** · the **smart bundle**: one decision, same team, estate to heirloom. **$1,420** = **$1,775** market stack minus a real **$355 thank-you** for ordering early. **Only published courtesy.**
4. **Feel:** Better than stitching vendors elsewhere · warm Chalet · Mom cared for · editorial quality · **honest** pricing, not phantom discounts.

**Do not advertise** a fake compare. Card shows **$1,775** market stack → **$1,420** pre-order → **$355 thank-you** (real).

**Backend:** Higher retail + volume lab deals = margin on **$1,420** bundle without bargain-bin positioning.

## Mom Milestone · what it is (luxe, included)

**Not:** "Mom walkthrough," mini-session, add-on, or 15-minute bargain slot.  
**Is:** A **curated family heirloom moment** on the estate · included in $1,420.

### Ops definition (~15 minutes per senior)

- **When:** After senior is in full HMU and mid/late in the estate session (exact flow set with photographer).
- **Where:** One **curated vignette** (pond, Chalet porch, woodland edge · photographer picks).
- **Who:** Senior + Mom (default); siblings or Dad welcome if present and unhurried.
- **How:** Award-winning portrait photographer · **retouch team led by** a Colorado best-in-state yearbook editor (Fairview High, **Boulder, CO**).
- **Deliverable:** Included in **20+ retouched digitals** · Mom Milestone images are part of the gallery, not a separate SKU.
- **Tone:** Warm, guided, editorial · "this frame goes on your wall and her dorm," not "quick group shot."
