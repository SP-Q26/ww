## Live view · projector room (Producer-owned · photogs stay untethered on Z1/Z3)

**Principle:** Tether must **never** cost a zone photographer movement, framing, or attention. **Z1** (Look 3 + mom) and **Z3** (Look 1 · estate roam) are **RAW-only on body** — no cables, no laptop duty.

### Where live feed actually happens

| Leg | Location | Who shoots | Feed destination |
|-----|----------|------------|------------------|
| **Look 1 hook** | **Salon glam exit** (2–3 min mark) | **Producer / PA** · fixed light + tripod | **Projector room** (+ optional Chalet Side B mirror) |
| **Primary live tether** | **Z2 indoor · Look 2 shoot bay** | **Z2 photog** · **wireless only** (CamRanger / Air / equivalent) | **Projector room** · **12 min** · moms see **Look 2** big |
| **Afternoon prep** | Same **Z2 bay** (3:30–4:50) | Z2 photog or PA | Look 1 preview · same **projector** feed |
| **Z3 · Z1 · golden** | Outdoors / mom walks | Zone photogs | **No live tether** · PA may push **curated stills** to projector between triads if needed |

**Why Z2 indoor:** Shoot bay is **fixed** (seamless / window / one setup). Wireless TX on camera → laptop on **rolling cart at bay mouth** (not on the photog’s hip). **PA or Producer** runs laptop → **HDMI to projector room** — photog never touches the laptop mid-session.

**Why projector room (clutch):** Dark, cinematic, **big frame** — reads “event,” not lobby TV. Same room doubles as **rain editorial** and **live view** so moms aren’t crowding the Chalet fireplace. Chalet **Side B** can **mirror** the feed (HDMI split / wireless RX) for moms who stay on grazing · **projector = hero live view**.

**Why not Z3:** Look 1 is the **most mobile** leg (meadow, paths, carts). Cables fight the shot; tether UI fights the moment.

**Why not Z1 / lead:** Mom Milestone and Look 3 need **lead full attention** at Chalet-adjacent / outdoor spots — same rule as before.
