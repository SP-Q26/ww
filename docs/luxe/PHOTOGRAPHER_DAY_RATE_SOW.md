## Scope of work

1. Arrive **by 8:55 AM** (photographers) · remain through release unless Producer dismisses early.  
2. Operate **one assigned zone** · seniors rotate per Producer schedule.  
3. Capture **senior portraits** (and **mom moments** when the flow routes mom to your zone per call sheet).  
4. Provide **own camera body, lenses, cards, and batteries** for the full day.  
5. **No post-production:** no culling, retouching, album design, gallery upload, or client delivery.  
6. **No client management:** refer families to Producer staff.  
7. Professional conduct in salon, Chalet, and on grounds.

**All zone leads:** Consistent framing and exposure · sufficient coverage per senior session · reflector use when provided · folder standard below.
