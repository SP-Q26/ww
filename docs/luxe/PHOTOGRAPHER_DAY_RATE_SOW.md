## Scope of work

1. Arrive at call time · remain through release unless Producer dismisses early.  
2. Operate **one assigned zone** · seniors rotate to you per Producer schedule.  
3. Capture **senior portraits** per zone assignment · **Lead only:** Mom Milestone at Chalet as each senior’s **final leg** (~15 min with mom).  
4. Provide **own camera body, lenses, cards, and batteries** for a full experience day (up to 24 seniors site-wide).  
5. **No post-production:** no culling, retouching, album design, gallery upload, or client delivery.  
6. **No client management:** no pricing, packages, upsells, or schedule changes. Refer families to Producer staff.  
7. Professional conduct in salon, Chalet, and on grounds.

### Lead zone photographer (additional)

- **Zone 1 (Chalet-adjacent):** Look 3 + **Mom Milestone** finale with mom · portable lighting.  
- **Zone + production lift:** pacing, quality bar for the crew · escalate ops to Producer.  
- **No Chalet tether duty** (live view is Z2 bay + PA · not lead).  
- **Folder standard** for all three drives (Producer approves).

### Zone photographers (standard)

- Consistent framing and exposure · **sufficient RAW coverage for a ~20 min session** per senior (target **50–80+ frames** unless Producer directs otherwise).  
- Reflector use if provided on set.
