## Why this matters (good → great)

**Good** is the estate, the team, the album table, Mom not waiting in the car. **Great** is what she cannot name when she walks out: the room felt *considered*. Low volume. A line from Joni in under three minutes. Firelight and mocktails while nobody sold her anything.

Moms will not cite the playlist. They will say *“they thought of everything”* or *“I cried and I don’t know why.”* Competitors can copy a price or a backdrop. They rarely copy **taste under the surface** · offline, private, no streaming, no lobby TV. This is the whisper that separates you from studios that are merely competent.

                              │     stiff boards · sleek covers
                              │     approach from BOTH ends

### Two screens · two jobs (do not combine)

| Display | Job | Content |
|---------|-----|---------|
| **Live view** | “Where is my kid right now?” | **Projector room** (hero) · **Salon glam publish** + **Z2 Look 2 bay** wireless · optional **Chalet Side B** mirror · no playlist on projector |
| **Ambience TV** | Mood · snippets · MV filler | Pi `chalet-lounge.m3u` · low volume · peripheral |

Mixing both on one TV breaks both experiences.

### Where the ambience TV goes

**Recommended: Side A · seating/kitchen wall · high mount · angled toward sofa, not the fire.**

| Placement | Verdict |
|-----------|---------|
| **Above fireplace (center)** | ❌ Steals the wrap · reads “TV room” not Chalet |
| **Live-view side (Side B)** | ❌ Fights tether · moms want the kid, not Joni |
| **Over king bed collection** | ❌ Competes with tactile album moment |
| **Side A · long wall above/ beside seating · ~7–8′ high** | ✅ Fire stays hero on that side · grazers + seated moms catch light/motion · snippets overheard from kitchen |

**Mount:** offset **30–45°** from fireplace sightline so no one stares at it straight on. Sound toward seating/kitchen, **not** blasted at collection bed.

### Where live view goes (canon)

**Primary: Projector room** — laptop → **projector** · dim lights · moms drift in for 30–60 sec · same room as moody rain/editorial · **clutch** because the image is large and separate from Chalet noise.

**Optional mirror: Chalet Side B** — small monitor/TV · same HDMI split or wireless RX from PA laptop · for moms who won’t leave the fireplace.

- **Not** on the ambience TV (Side A) · **not** meadow tether on Z3  
- **No audio** on live view or very low · ambience TV carries room sound  

**Staff:** “Your daughter’s on the projector when she’s in Look 2” · no megaphone · optional lean rail at projector door.

### Flow for 6–8 moms (first wave)

| Zone | Who uses it |
|------|-------------|
| Kitchen + Side A seating | Graze · talk · overhear snippets |
| Fireplace wrap | Warmth · pairs split across both sides |
| Side B live view | Peek at senior on estate |
| King bed display | Swatches · Chalet collection · `/order` tablet nearby |
| Bathroom | Primp · reset |

**Staff:** never point at the ambience TV. Live view is optional peek only · no “your daughter’s on screen!” announcements.

## Chalet lounge · first wave (6 moms)

**Capacity note:** First intake is **6 moms** together. The screen is **not** a six-person cinema. It is **shared ambience** while they:

- graze and mocktails  
- watch the fireplace  
- handle album / swatch table (collection watching)  
- slip to the **private bathroom** to primp  
- drift in and out of conversation  

**Interview clips will land hard** · that is the point. Moms who carried the day hear women they admire speak with honesty. Expect tears, laughter, quiet nods · not everyone facing the TV at once. **Good filler** in the gaps between hosted moments · never competes with a host or sales conversation.

**Clip format:** **under 1–3 minutes** each · **MTV-style snippets** (punchy cuts, not documentary sit-downs). Glanceable · a single line can stop someone mid-mocktail · gone before the room has to “watch a show.”

### Room behavior (design for parallel attention)

| Mom | Likely focus | Screen role |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| 1–2 | Fireplace / sofa talk | Hear interview · glance up when a line hits |
| 1–2 | Swatch table · album samples | Audio carries · visual optional |
| 1 | Bathroom / primp | Returns mid-clip · feels continuous not “starting a show” |
| 0–1 | Actually watching | Full clip · emotional peak |

**Volume:** low enough that **two moms can talk at the mocktail table** without shouting. Interviews should feel overheard, not presented.

**Display placement:** offset from swatch table and fireplace sightlines · not the room’s only focal point. Fireplace + table + grazing beat one screen.

### Chalet playlist arc (first 45 min · 6-mom wave)

Program for **overlap**, not a captive audience:

| Block | ~min | Content | Why |
|-------|-----:|---------|-----|
| **Arrival** | 8–12 | Ambient + instrumental (Kelly Moran, Lisa Gerrard beds) | Room fills · no talking head on entry |
| **Warm** | 10–15 | 1–2 music films (Sade, Maria Gadu live) | Taste signal · low emotional ask |
| **Snippet** | 1–3 | Joni or Badu · MTV-style cut | Lands after mocktails in hand · one killer line |
| **Breath** | 5–8 | Ambient / fireplace b-roll you own | Bathroom runs · table conversation |
| **Snippet** | 1–3 | Different voice · motherhood / craft | Hits the mom who just sat down |
| **Music** | 8–12 | Frou Frou · Melody Gardot · MV | Downtime filler |
| **Loop** | — | Repeat with shuffle inside blocks | No one notices a “restart” |

**Rule:** never **back-to-back snippets**. Always ambient or music between. Clips stay **≤3 min** · stack **4–6 snippets per hour** max if spaced with MVs/ambient (not a wall of talking heads).

### Interview curation (Chalet-only)

**MTV / snippet edit** · one idea per clip · hard in, hard out. Prioritize moments where icons speak about **craft, motherhood, patience, becoming** · not scandal or hot takes. Think *quote that rewires you*, not *episode to follow*.

| Artist | Chalet use |
|--------|------------|
| Joni Mitchell | Literary mom energy · legacy |
| Erykah Badu | Warmth · wit · depth |
| Maria Gadu | European intimacy · live session feel |
| Lisa Gerrard | Ethereal breath between conversations |
| Sade | Silk · unhurried |
| Kelly Moran | Modern ambient · no lyrics distraction |

Keep MHU salon **lighter on interviews** · Chalet carries the emotional weight.

### Staff cue (first wave)

- Screen **on before door opens** · already in ambient block  
- **No** “we’re starting a video” · it is already the room  
- If a mom is visibly moved · staff **does not** explain or sell · offer water, graze, silence  
- Host owns conversation · screen never talks over a human

## Mounting · MHU (mirror question)

**Do not** rely on reversed-through-mirror as the primary view.

| Approach | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| CRT reversed behind mirror, watch through glass | Cinematic on paper · hard to read · two-way mirror film costly · heat behind mirror · neck strain if they try to “watch” |
| **Recommended: side or high-corner mount** | CRT glow + color spill **in the mirror periphery** = the vibe without legible content battle |
| High behind chair (not through mirror) | Soft wash on wall/ceiling · senior feels atmosphere, HMU artist keeps sightlines |

**Play:** 13–14″ CRT **on a side wall** or **high rear corner**, angled slightly toward mirror so reflection picks up **light and motion**, not subtitles. Volume low · interviews sparse in MHU.

## Playback stack

```
USB SSD → Pi 5 → mpv --fs --loop-playlist playlist.m3u
              → 3.5mm → BT transmitter → speaker
              → composite/VGA (MHU) or HDMI (Chalet) → display
```

- **No Wi‑Fi** required on shoot day  
- Encode: H.264 MP4, 720p max, normalize ~−16 LUFS  
- Folders: `01-ambient/` · `02-music/` · `03-clips/`
